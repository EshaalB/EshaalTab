# EshaalTab Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 31 July 2026
**Applies to:** EshaalTab — New Tab & Bookmark Boards

## Summary

EshaalTab does not sell user data and does not transmit personal data to the developer. It has no developer-operated account system, backend server, advertising, analytics or telemetry.

The extension processes information locally when providing its new-tab workspace features. This may include saved links, bookmark information, open-tab information, browsing-history search results, notes, tasks, appearance settings, uploaded wallpapers and an optional display name.

Optional features may send limited information directly to third-party services. For example, the weather feature sends the user-selected city and derived coordinates to Open-Meteo. Remote favicon and wallpaper features may request resources from the relevant external providers.

## Information processed locally

EshaalTab may locally process and store:

* EshaalTab boards, saved links, tags and pinned links
* Chrome bookmark titles and URLs imported at the user’s request
* Open-tab titles and URLs used for local searching and tab stashing
* Browsing-history titles and URLs shown in user-requested Ctrl+K searches
* Notes, clipboard snippets, tasks and Pomodoro session counts
* Appearance, layout, font and cursor settings
* User-uploaded image and video wallpapers
* An optional display name
* An optional weather city

This information is not sent to the developer.

A limited subset of appearance settings may be stored through `chrome.storage.sync`. Chrome performs this syncing through the user’s Google account. EshaalTab does not operate or access a separate synchronization server.
