EshaalTab turns the blank new tab into a page worth opening.

Your links, grouped the way you think about them. Notes and a focus timer where
you already are. A wallpaper that makes the whole thing yours.

WHAT IT DOES

• Bookmark boards — group links into colour-coded boards. Give each one its own
  colour; the board takes it, and stays readable in light and dark.
• Pinned links — the handful you open every day, one click from the new tab.
• Quick save — save the current page from the toolbar button, the right-click
  menu, or Alt+Shift+S.
• Find anything — Ctrl+K searches your saved links. Grant the optional
  permission and it searches open tabs and history too.
• Notes — a real notepad with headings, lists and checklists. Tick a box and the
  task appears on your new tab.
• Focus timer — a full-screen countdown with presets, and a session count.
• Make it yours — image or video wallpapers with crop, blur, dimming and edge
  shading; accent colours; corner style; typeface; and how solid every surface
  should be.

ABOUT SEARCH

The search box hands your query to the search provider you have already chosen
in your browser. EshaalTab does not change your search settings. You can send a
single search somewhere else — Google, DuckDuckGo, Yandex, Yahoo, Brave,
Perplexity, ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, YouTube, Reddit, Pinterest or Quora — by picking it in the bar; that choice is stored
locally and applies only to searches you start from this page.

YOUR DATA

Boards, notes, tasks, settings and uploaded wallpapers are stored in your
browser. There is no account, no advertising, no analytics, and no server of
ours to send anything to.

Optional permissions are requested only when you use the feature that needs
them: bookmarks for a one-off import, tabs and history for Ctrl+K.

Open source: https://github.com/EshaalB/EshaalTab

## Screenshots — the five to take

Take these at 1280×800 with the browser zoomed to 100%. Seed the state first so
nothing looks empty; a screenshot of an empty product sells an empty product.

1. **Home, in use.** Wallpaper up, clock centred, five pinned links with real
   favicons, two or three to-dos underneath. This is the one people judge on.
2. **Boards.** Four boards, each a different colour, each with three or four
   real links. Shows the customisation and the organising idea at once.
3. **Notes.** A note with a heading, a couple of bullets and two checklist items
   — one ticked. Clipboard panel visible on the right.
4. **Settings.** The Appearance page, sidebar visible. This is what says
   "genuinely customisable" rather than "one theme".
5. **Focus timer.** Full-screen countdown mid-session.

Avoid: near-black screenshots, tiny unreadable UI, empty states, and the
save-bookmark popup as a headline shot — it is the least interesting surface and
currently occupies slot 4.
