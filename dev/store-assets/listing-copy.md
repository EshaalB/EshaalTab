## Description

---

EshaalTab replaces the new tab with a calm page for the things you open every day.

• Boards: group links into boards and give each one a colour. Pin your favourite boards to Home.
• Search: search the web from the bar, or press Ctrl+K to find links, open tabs, notes and history.
• Notes: a simple notepad with lists and checklists that stays in sync across your tabs.
• Focus timer: a full-screen countdown with 15, 25, 45 and 60 minute presets.
• Library: save a window as a session, keep a read-later list, and restore workspaces in one click.
• Your look: image or video wallpapers, light and dark mode, accent colours and fonts.

Private by default. Everything is stored in your browser. There is no account, no ads and no tracking. Optional permissions (tabs, bookmarks, history) are only requested when you turn on the feature that needs them.

The search bar uses your browser's default search engine unless you pick another one in the bar.

Import bookmarks from Chrome, or from a CSV or JSON file.

Free and open source: https://github.com/EshaalB/EshaalTab
